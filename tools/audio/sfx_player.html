Load PCFF001.TIM and PCFF001.DIG to list the 30 samples (whistle, crowd, checks, posts, organ hits and more).

Format: DIG holds signed 8-bit mono PCM; each 32-byte TIM record gives the sample id, rate (11025 or 22050 Hz), length and optional loop. Full notes in docs/SFX_FORMAT.md of the NHL94PC disassembly.
